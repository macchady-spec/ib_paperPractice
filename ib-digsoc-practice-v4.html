How to Use This Tool

IB Digital Society Exam Practice Studio — Annotation, Rubric & Self-Assessment

1

Select your paper and question type

Use Paper 1 / Paper 2 to switch papers, then click the question type — 2pt, 6pt, 8pt for Paper 1 or 2pt, 4pt, 6pt, 12pt for Paper 2. The rubric, checklist and score calculator update automatically.

2

Upload your answer

Click Upload ↑ to choose a PDF, photo, or screenshot of your answer. Google Docs → File → Download → PDF document. Handwritten? Take a clear photo.

3

Annotate with stamps

Click a stamp button to activate it, then click anywhere on your answer to place it. Press ESC to cancel. Drag stamps to reposition. Double-click × to remove one.

4

Use the Checklist & Score tabs

Switch to Checklist to tick every element present in your answer. Switch to Score to see your estimated band — and to check whether a hard cap applies (one-sided response, categorical conclusion, or source issues).

5

Export your work

Click ↓ Export. A single self-contained HTML file downloads with your answer, stamps, checklist ticks, notes, and score — everything preserved. Re-open it to continue editing or share with your teacher.

💾 About Exporting

Exported files work offline — no internet needed. Your image is embedded. You can re-export to update.

⚠ About the Rubrics & Scores

The 8pt (P1c) and 12pt (P2 Q4) band descriptors use official IB wording. The 6pt (P1b) uses the IB 4-band markband. The 2pt and 4pt questions use point-based marking. Score estimates are indicative — always verify with your teacher.
